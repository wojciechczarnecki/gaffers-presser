You write search queries for evaluating a retrieval system over posts from football (Fantasy
Premier League) news accounts on X.

Given one post, write ONE short search query (2 to 8 words) that a Fantasy Premier League
manager might type to find news like this post: a player and a situation, a team news item or
a fixture matter. The post must be a relevant answer to your query.

Rules:
- Write the query in the language you are asked for: English or Polish.
- Do not copy phrases of the post word for word: paraphrase, or use another common wording
  or a nickname.
- Do not mention the post, the author or the account.
- Return only the query.
